## Research (facts used)

- In 2025 (BE 2568) Bangkok City Hall (กทม.) collected **391,027** krathongs from 110 sites, 24% fewer than the year
  before. **Foam: 28,298 (7.23%)**; natural materials 82.76%; bread 10.01%.
  Sources: [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/358272),
  [Siam Rath](https://siamrath.co.th/quality-of-life/news/108530),
  [Spacebar](https://spacebar.th/social/sustainability-bangkok-krathong-2025-eco-report).
- City staff start collecting from the canals, parks and the Chao Phraya on the night itself and work through to
  dawn (about 5 a.m.).
- Natural-material krathongs are ground up and sent to an organic-fertilizer plant; foam ones go to landfill (the city
  has estimated about 500 years for foam to break down).
  Sources: [Matichon](https://www.matichon.co.th/local/news_720776),
  [The Bangkok Insight](https://www.thebangkokinsight.com/467743/).
- Bread that fish do not eat rots and spoils the water. Krathongs that slip past the collection drift to the banks
  or downstream to the sea.
- Rounding: the narration says "เกือบสี่แสนใบ" (almost four hundred thousand) and the screen shows **391,027**; it says
  "ราวเจ็ดเปอร์เซ็นต์" (about 7%) and the screen shows **7%**. Both match the figures above.
