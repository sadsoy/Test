## Creative choice

Of all the issues in the brief, I chose **the conflict between production and purchasing power** as the backbone of
the story arc. It is the clearest twist ("then who buys?"), and the other issues follow from it naturally:
- who owns the machines;
- where income comes from (UBI, dividends);
- the role humans are left with.

The story goes: hook → facts as they stand today → **a clearly labelled hypothetical** → production booms →
**twist** → why the money loop breaks → where the money flows → ideas people are proposing → the real question →
a question back to the viewer.

## Facts checked (and kept separate from the hypothetical)

| Claim in the clip | Source |
|---|---|
| About 1 in 4 jobs worldwide is exposed to generative AI, and **most are more likely to be transformed than replaced**; clerical work is the most exposed | [ILO and NASK, *Generative AI and Jobs: A Refined Global Index*, 20 May 2025](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/one-four-jobs-risk-being-transformed-genai-new-ilo%E2%80%93nask-global-index-shows) |
| WEF **projects** that by 2030 there will be 170 million new jobs and 92 million lost (a net gain of 78 million) | [WEF Future of Jobs Report 2025, Jan 2025](https://www.weforum.org/press/2025/01/future-of-jobs-report-2025-78-million-new-job-opportunities-by-2030-but-urgent-upskilling-needed-to-prepare-workforces/) |
| Finland tested a basic income in 2017–18: 2,000 unemployed people received €560 a month; employment effects were **small**, but participants felt less stress and more financial security | [Ministry of Social Affairs and Health / Kela, final report, 6 May 2020](https://stm.fi/en/-/perustulokokeilun-tulokset-tyollisyysvaikutukset-vahaisia-toimeentulo-ja-psyykkinen-terveys-koettiin-paremmaksi) |
| For most people, the money they spend comes from wages (the production → wages → spending loop) | Basic macroeconomics (the circular flow of income), used to frame the hypothetical |

What the clip does **not** do, per the brief:
- It does not say that everyone will lose their job: it opens with "วันนี้ยังไม่ถึงขั้นนั้น" (we are not there yet today).
- It names no year for "the robots take over"; the only year is WEF's 2030, labelled as a projection.
- It invents no statistics.
- It does not exaggerate what AI can do today.
- The fiction is stamped **"สถานการณ์สมมติ · WHAT IF"** (a hypothetical) before it starts.
- UBI and dividends are labelled "* แนวคิดที่ยังถกเถียงกันอยู่ ไม่ใช่คำตอบสำเร็จรูป" (ideas still under debate, not
  ready-made answers).

## 2. Storyboard

| Scene | Time (s) | Picture and movement | Transition |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 Hook | 0–4.5 | A factory at night, with a conveyor of boxes. A **worker** (silhouette) packs boxes. The **robot arm** switches on, and the worker turns, a paper cutout flipping round, and walks aside. A **humanoid robot** walks in and takes over at the belt. "ดีใจ?" pops in green, then "กลัว?" in coral, and the lights dim to red. | Torn paper sheet (dark → cream paper) |
| S2 Facts today | 4.5–12.2 | The "ข้อเท็จจริงวันนี้" desk. **4 job cards** (office, nurse, builder, teacher) slide in; one card lights up with an "AI" badge (**1 in 4** made visible), "≈ 1 ใน 4 งานทั่วโลก ได้รับผลจาก AI · ILO พ.ค. 2025". The camera dives into that card: its **4 tasks**, where "เอกสาร" (documents) and "ตารางนัด" (schedules) turn mint and switch to a robot icon, while "คุยกับคน" (talking with people) and "ตัดสินใจ" (decisions) stay with the person. **"เปลี่ยนรูป ≠ หายไป"**, plus the WEF 2030 projection. | Torn paper sheet (to purple) |
| S3 Hypothetical | 12.2–18.0 | The **"สถานการณ์สมมติ · WHAT IF"** stamp. A long factory with 5 robot arms; the camera trucks right as the conveyor and the arms speed up and boxes pile into a mountain ("ผลิตได้มหาศาล"). A price tag swings in with a falling arrow ("ราคาถูกลง"). | Continuous camera |
| S4 Twist | 18.0–19.2 | The camera **pulls back and up** through the doorway: the boxes are piled high, the street is empty, the shop has no customers, and a big "?". **The music drops out** to make the moment land. "แล้วใครจะซื้อ?" | Dissolve |
| S5–S7 The money loop | 19.2–30.8 | A ring diagram: โรงงาน (factory) → ค่าจ้าง (wages) → คน (people) → ซื้อของ (spending) → โรงงาน, with coins travelling round.
