It is a small research tokamak donated by ASIPP (China), used for training.

## Story arc

| Stage | Content |
|---|---|
| Hook | The Sun shines because it fuses atoms. **"Could we light that fire on Earth?"** A spark crosses from the Sun to the Earth. |
| Setup | Fusion joins light nuclei; fission (today's power plants) splits heavy ones. The plasma must be about **10 times hotter than the Sun's core**, held by magnets (a tokamak). |
| Escalation (fact) | **Dec 2022:** for the first time the reaction gave out more energy than the laser put in, **but the facility as a whole still drew more electricity**. |
| Turning point (hypothetical) | **"สถานการณ์สมมติ"** (a hypothetical): net electricity lights a city. The fuel comes from seawater and lithium, with no carbon while generating. The unexpected payoff: **abundant energy might turn seawater into fresh water for a whole city**. |
| Reality check | "ไม่ใช่เวทมนตร์" (it isn't magic): reactor parts become radioactive, costs are high, and nobody knows when it will be ready. |
| Conclusion | "เรายังสร้างดวงอาทิตย์ไม่ได้ แต่กำลังเรียนรู้วิธีจุดไฟแบบเดียวกัน" (we can't build a sun yet, but we are learning to light the same fire), with a thread of light between the Sun and the Earth and the title as a question. |

## Facts checked

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Tokamak plasma ≈ 150 million °C, about 10 times hotter than the Sun's core (≈ 15 million °C); ITER aims for Q = 10 | ITER (iter.org); NASA (the Sun's core) |
| NIF, 5 Dec 2022: 3.15 MJ out from 2.05 MJ of laser energy in (target gain > 1). The facility's lasers drew far more energy from the grid, so there was **no net electricity**. The record so far is 7 Apr 2025: 8.6 MJ from 2.08 MJ (gain 4.13). | [LLNL: Achieving Fusion Ignition](https://lasers.llnl.gov/science/pursuit-of-ignition) |
| ITER, 2024 plan: research operations start in 2034 and D-T in 2039. ITER is an experimental machine and **does not produce electricity**. | [ITER baseline 2024](https://www.iter.org/node/28514), [Physics World](https://physicsworld.com/a/iter-fusion-reactor-hit-by-massive-decade-long-delay-and-e5bn-price-hike/) |
| Waste: neutrons make structural materials radioactive and tritium is radioactive. Most fusion waste is low- or intermediate-level, with no transuranic elements and less decay heat than fission waste, but it is **not zero**, and some components may stay radioactive for a long time. | [Nuclear Fusion 2022 (IAEA co-authors)](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1741-4326/57/9/092003), [UKAEA, Bailey et al. 2021](https://scientific-publications.ukaea.uk/wp-content/uploads/BAILEY_2021_NUCL-_FUSION_61_036010-1.PDF) |
| Fuel: deuterium from seawater; tritium is bred from lithium; the reaction emits no CO2 while generating. There is no runaway chain reaction (the plasma simply goes out when disturbed). | ITER / EUROfusion (standard material) |
| The Sun fuses hydrogen into helium through the proton–proton chain; reactors use D-T | Standard physics (the picture is labelled "ไฮโดรเจน → ฮีเลียม"; the Earth panel shows D + T → He-4 + n) |

Rules from the brief and how the clip meets them:
- **Fusion is not described as waste-free:** card 1 says "ส่วนใหญ่อายุสั้นกว่ากากฟิชชัน แต่ไม่ใช่ศูนย์" (mostly shorter-lived than fission waste, but not zero).
- **It never claims we built a sun:** the ending says "ยังสร้างไม่ได้" (we can't yet).
- **Fusion and fission are kept apart:** each has its own panel and its own definition.
- **The lab reaction is not equated with commercial power:** a third bar shows what the whole facility uses.
- **No year for commercial plants:** only ITER's 2039 D-T date, labelled as a plan that doesn't generate electricity.
- **Facts and the hypothetical are separated:** the "สถานการณ์สมมติ · WHAT IF" stamp comes before the future part, and the fresh-water idea is introduced with "อาจ" (may).

Production problems and how they were solved:
1. The first take was 64 s, so the script was cut twice, keeping every caveat.
2. The TTS **refused one request on SAFETY grounds** because of the phrase "กากกัมมันตรังสี" (radioactive waste). Rewording it to "ชิ้นส่วนเครื่องกลายเป็นสารกัมมันตรังสี" (reactor parts become radioactive) kept the facts and went through.
3. The day's 3.1 quota ran out, so the voice switched to 2.5-flash-preview-tts.
4. `align.py` dropped the final phrase, and the risers in `audio.mjs` were silently disabled by a comment. Both were fixed here and in the skill's kit.
