## Facts checked (on 23 Nov 2026)

| Topic | Source and what it says |
|---|---|
| Current situation | [NOAA CPC, ENSO Diagnostic Discussion, 12 Nov 2026](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso_advisory/ensodisc.shtml): **an El Niño Advisory is in effect** and the event is strengthening. There is a **>79%** chance of a strong to very strong El Niño through Jan–Mar 2027, and about a 60% chance of a historic event. The next update is 10 Dec 2026. |
| International view | [WMO](https://wmo.int/topics/el-nino-la-nina): El Niño established since mid-2026; a 20 Nov 2026 update is headlined "El Niño set to become very strong, raising risks of extreme weather into 2027". WMO stresses that **impacts vary by region**. |
| Definition | El Niño is the warm phase of ENSO: sea-surface temperatures in the central and eastern tropical Pacific are above normal. "Super El Niño" is an **informal name for a very strong El Niño**, not a different phenomenon. |
| Mechanism | The trade winds normally push warm water west. In El Niño they weaken, warm surface water spreads east, and the upwelling of cold water off South America weakens. |
| Impacts | These are tendencies, not certainties: Indonesia and Australia often turn drier than normal; the coasts of Peru and Ecuador and the southern US (in winter) often turn wetter. Each event differs. |
| Global warming | Long-term global warming and El Niño are separate things. El Niño temporarily lifts the global average temperature on top of the long-term rise, so the two together raise the chance of extremes. |

What the clip does **not** do, per the brief:
- It does not say Thailand will certainly face a severe drought (Thailand is not singled out).
- It invents no numbers: the strength gauge and legend use words, and only "คาดการณ์ · NOAA/WMO (พ.ย. 2569)" (forecast) appears on screen.
- It does not tell a forecast as if it had happened: the narration says "มีแนวโน้ม" (tends to), "มัก" (often), "อาจ" (may) and "แล้วแต่ภูมิภาค" (depends on the region).
